# Deck box (82 × 75)

A two-piece clamshell deck box for double-sleeved Commander decks (Dragon Shield sleeves).
No hinge, magnet or latch: the two halves close over each other and hold by friction.

| File | Part | Height | Fit tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `deckbox-82x75-top.stl` | Lid | 61.0 mm | 0.05 mm gap |
| `deckbox-82x75-bottom.stl` | Base | 68.9 mm | 0.1 mm gap, 0.8 mm bevel on the lower edge |

## Dimensions (measured from the meshes)

- Outer footprint: **82 × 75 mm**
- Wall thickness: **3 mm**
- Inner cavity: **76 × 69 mm**: 76 mm of stack depth, 69 mm across the card's width
- Both meshes are watertight and ready to slice.
